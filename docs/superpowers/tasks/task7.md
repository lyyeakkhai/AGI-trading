==================================================
1. PRODUCT CONTEXT
==================================================

This is a private, single-user AI trading intelligence platform.

Hermes is the Main Trading Agent.

The product flow is:

Market Data
    ↓
Hermes Analysis
    ↓
Opportunity Detection
    ↓
Trade Proposal
    ↓
Deterministic Risk Validation
    ↓
Owner Approval
    ↓
Execution

Task 07 implements the Trade Proposal stage.

IMPORTANT:

A Trade Proposal is NOT an executed trade.

Hermes proposes.

The Risk Engine validates.

The owner approves.

Execution happens later.

For this frontend task:
- use deterministic mock data
- do not connect to Binance
- do not execute trades
- do not implement real risk calculations
- do not implement real AI
- do not implement real approval backend
